### 3. Sidebar Actions

- **Click ➕** → Create a new conversation
- **Click any thread** → Switch to that conversation
- **Click 🗑️** → Delete that conversation
- **Click outside sidebar** → Close the sidebar

## Troubleshooting

**Can't see the ☰ icon?**
- It should be at the very top-left of the screen
- Look for three horizontal lines (hamburger icon)
- It's in the AppBar next to the conversation title

**Sidebar not opening?**
- Make sure you're clicking the ☰ icon
- Try refreshing the page (Ctrl+R or F5)
- Check browser console for errors

**No conversations in sidebar?**
- If you just started, you'll see "No conversations yet"
- Send a message to create your first conversation
- Then click ☰ to see it in the sidebar
