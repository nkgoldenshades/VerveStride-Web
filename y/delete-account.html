Delete Your Account

⚠️ Important Warning

Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone. All your data will be permanently removed from our servers.

What will be deleted:

  • Your profile information (name, age, gender, height, weight)
  • All activity tracking data (workouts, runs, walks, etc.)
  • Meal logs and nutrition tracking history
  • Water intake logs
  • Streak data and achievements
  • Calendar events and progress history
  • All personal settings and preferences

How to Delete Your Account:

1

Open the VerveStride app on your device

2

Go to the Profile tab (bottom navigation)

3

Tap the Settings icon (⚙️) in the top right corner

4

Scroll down to the Account section

5

Tap "Delete Account"

6

Confirm your decision by tapping "Delete" in the confirmation dialog

7

Your account and all associated data will be permanently deleted within 24 hours

Data Retention:

After account deletion:

  • Your data will be permanently removed from our servers within 24 hours
  • Some anonymized analytics data may be retained for service improvement
  • Backup copies will be deleted within 30 days as per our backup retention policy
  • You will be immediately logged out from all devices

Need Help?

If you're having trouble deleting your account or have questions about data deletion, please contact our support team at vervestride.app@gmail.com
