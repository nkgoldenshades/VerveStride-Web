## 💡 Platform Capabilities

### 1. **AI Assistant Core** 🤖
- **Conversational AI**: Multi-turn conversations with context memory
- **Multi-modal Input**: Text, voice, images, documents (PDF, DOCX, etc.)
- **Web Search Integration**: Real-time information access
- **Voice Activation**: Hands-free "VerveStride AI" wake word
- **Document Analysis**: Upload and analyze any document
- **Live Video Analysis**: Real-time visual understanding

### 2. **Creative AI Studio** 🎨
- **Image Generation**: AI-generated images from text prompts
  - Google Vertex AI (Imagen)
  - Replicate (SDXL)
  - OpenAI (DALL-E 3)
- **Video Generation**: Create AI videos (up to 10 seconds)
- **Music/Audio Generation**: AI-composed music and soundscapes
- **Automatic Fallback**: Multi-provider system ensures reliability

### 3. **Smart Reminders & Alarms** ⏰
- **4 Alarm Modes**:
  - 🔔 Normal (default sound)
  - 🎵 Custom MP3
  - 🤖 AI Voice (personalized wake messages)
  - 🤖+🎵 AI Voice + Music combo
- **Flexible Scheduling**: Once, daily, weekly, custom days
- **AI Wake Messages**: Personalized greetings based on time and style
- **Universal Compatibility**: Works on all Android 5.0+ devices

### 4. **Fitness & Wellness Hub** 💪
- **Workout Tracking**: Pose detection with 17 body joints
- **Movement Analysis**: Real-time tracking of most-moved body parts
- **Meal Logging**: Calorie and macro tracking
- **Progress Tracking**: Long-term fitness analytics
- **AI Coaching**: Personalized workout and nutrition guidance

### 5. **Productivity Tools** 📊
- **Goal Management**: Set, track, and achieve goals with AI support
- **Habit Tracking**: Build and maintain healthy habits
- **Analytics Dashboard**: Visualize progress across all areas
- **Multi-platform**: Web, Android (iOS coming soon)
