# 🎨 AI Creative Tools - User Guide

## How to Access Image & Video Generation

### Step-by-Step Guide

1. **Open the App**
   - Launch VerveStride

2. **Go to Settings**
   - Tap the **Profile/Settings** icon in the bottom navigation bar

3. **Open AI Settings**
   - Scroll down and tap **AI Settings**

4. **Find AI Creative Tools**
   - Scroll down to the **"AI Creative Tools"** section
   - You'll see two options:
     - 🎨 **Image Generation** (20 credits)
     - 🎬 **Video Generation** (50 credits)

5. **Generate Content**
   - Tap the tool you want to use
   - Enter your prompt
   - Tap "Generate"
   - Wait for AI to create your content

## Tips for Best Results

### Image Generation:
- ✅ Be specific about what you want
- ✅ Mention style (professional, modern, clean, etc.)
- ✅ Include lighting preferences
- ✅ Specify background (clean, white, gym, etc.)
- ❌ Avoid vague prompts like "make something cool"

### Video Generation:
- ✅ Describe the movement clearly
- ✅ Mention the perspective (side view, front view)
- ✅ Include pace (slow, fast, smooth)
- ✅ Specify duration (5-30 seconds)
- ❌ Don't request complex multi-person scenes

## Troubleshooting

### "Not enough credits"
- Check your credit balance in Settings → Credits
- Claim your daily bonus (5 free credits)
- Purchase credits or upgrade to Pro/Elite

### "Generation failed"
- Try a simpler prompt
- Check your internet connection
- Make sure you have enough credits
- Try again in a few minutes

### "Feature not showing"
- Update to the latest version
- Make sure you're logged in
- Check Settings → AI Settings → AI Creative Tools
